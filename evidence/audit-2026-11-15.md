## Verified (PROVEN, command run or source read)

- Live bytecode equals the built artifact: 0 differing bytes outside the immutable slots.
- No double count: shMonad deletes a completed request before paying (`consumeMaturedRequests`), so `pendingMon()`
  never counts MON that has already arrived as WMON.
- No unbounded growth: shMonad keeps at most 7 pending slots per account, merged by completion epoch, plus one matured
  slot, so `totalAssets()` cannot become too expensive to read however many chunks the desk sends.
- EVC pattern matches Euler's `ERC4626EVCCollateral`: status check on the owner after transfer, transferFrom,
  withdraw, redeem and `redeemForWmon`; `_msgSender()` resolves the EVC's on-behalf account, so a controller's
  liquidation `transfer` moves the violator's shares.
- Reentrancy: `receive()` is empty, `redeemForWmon` burns before it pays, shMonad's calls are `nonReentrant`.
- Wiring on chain: the router resolves ushMON to shMON; the WMON vault lends 97/98 against it; the desk's only
  collateral is ushMON and its only controller the WMON vault; the two retired EulerSwap pools are no longer EVC
  operators of the desk.
- Risk shape: the loan is WMON and the collateral is a fixed MON amount from the moment of the request, so MON's
  price does not move the loan's health. Only shMonad's shMON/MON rate and the feed's lag do, and the rate moves
  about 0.02% a day.

## Findings

### M1. If shMonad freezes, the collateral is stuck and the WMON loan cannot be repaid
- What can go wrong: shMonad sets `closed` on a stake deficit (`StakeTracker.sol` 2957, 3042). `requestUnstake` and
  `completeUnstake` then revert, and `redeem` reverts too once the shMON is in the unstake.
- What it costs: the WMON loan stays open at up to 30% a year until shMonad reopens; a liquidator who seizes ushMON
  cannot turn it into anything.
- Call: accept as a dependency on shMonad, and keep the WMON vault's borrow cap at what we can carry through a freeze.
  Preconditions rare (a validator principal deficit, "impossible under current Monad staking assumptions" per Fastlane).

### L1. Anyone may deposit, and the operator may unstake everyone's shMON
- What can go wrong: `deposit`/`mint` are open. An outsider's shMON can be sent into the unstake by the desk, and
  an outsider can take the vault's WMON first with `redeemForWmon` before the desk settles.
- What it costs: no loss (pro rata at one share price), but the desk's settle finds less WMON and repays less that
  day; the rest waits another day. One day of WMON interest on the shortfall.
- Call: on the next deployment, restrict `deposit`/`mint` to the operator (override `maxDeposit`/`maxMint` to return
  0 for others). The contract has no proxy, so the live vaults keep the open deposit.

### L2. A chunk larger than shMonad's no-delay allowance waits 2 more epochs
- What can go wrong: `_calcUnstakeRequestCompletionEpoch` adds 2 epochs (about 8 hours) when the request exceeds
  `getMaxUnstakeRequestAmountWithoutDelay()`.
- What it costs: that chunk's loan runs about 28 hours instead of 20.
- Call: the bot reads the allowance before sizing a chunk and splits a chunk above it. Operational, in the bot, not the vault.

### L3. Liquidators receive a share that pays out a day later
- What can go wrong: a liquidation hands ushMON to the liquidator, who must wait for maturity, call
  `completeUnstake`, then `redeemForWmon`.
- What it costs: fewer liquidators bid, so a bad position may sit longer. Price risk is near zero (MON against MON).
- Call: accept; document for the WMON vault's lenders.

### I1. Native MON sent directly is not counted until the next `completeUnstake`
`totalAssets()` reads `wmon.balanceOf`, not `address(this).balance`, and `completeUnstake` wraps only the delta.
Wrap the whole balance after the call. Cosmetic.

### I2. `maxWithdraw`/`maxRedeem` report the full balance while the shMON is in the unstake
A caller trusting them reverts on the transfer. The desk does not use them. Override to the shMON actually held.

### I3. `redeemForWmon` emits no event
Add a `Withdraw`-style event so indexers see WMON leaving.

### I4. Trust assumptions
shMonad is an upgradeable proxy controlled by Fastlane; the vault's value and liquidity follow its code. The operator
(the desk proxy) is immutable, so a new desk needs a new ushMON. Floating pragma `^0.8.24`.
